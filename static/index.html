Hello! My name is Arun. I am also known as zer0c00l in IRC channels. I live in San Jose, California and work on OpenStack and Kubernetes, solving complex problems related to infrastructure as a service.

Programming is both my hobby and my profession, which means I spend a lot of time in front of a computer. I love working with Free and Open Source software, GNU/Linux, Python, and systems programming on Linux.

I was introduced to Free Software in college. My first distribution was Fedora 9, and I have been contributing to the Fedora Project since around 2009. I am a package maintainer and look after these applications for Fedora.

I occasionally visit colleges to speak about Free Software and GNU/Linux. If you would like me to speak at your college, shoot me an email.
